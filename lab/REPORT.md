## magisk28_denylist_hidepid_a14

> Magisk 28 on a modern Android 14 device (2SI, /debug_ramdisk), Zygisk + DenyList enforced
> for this app, no Shamiko. DenyList unmounts everything Magisk from our namespace; /proc is
> mounted hidepid=2 so other processes are invisible; /proc/1/mounts is not readable.
> This fixture documents what text rules CANNOT see — expect gaps.

- `props_bootloader`
  - `verifiedbootstate=orange`
  - `vbmeta=unlocked`

**Known gaps**
- DenyList: Magisk mounts are removed from our namespace and /proc/1/mounts is unreadable on A10+ — mount_denylist cannot fire
- hidepid=2: magiskd is invisible to ps/comm/status from an untrusted_app
- Only signals left: unlocked bootloader + hardware Key Attestation (IntegrityDetector) and on-device native probes
